The house list

Six services, one workroom. Start with cloth and cut, or bring a garment you already love.

Four quiet visits

No rush. No guesswork. A garment is born on paper, then chalk, then cloth.

01

Consult

Occasion, posture, and cloth. You leave with a plan and a ticket.

02

Measure

Twenty points, noted once and kept for every future order.

03

Baste

Still in chalk. We move seams until the garment sits.

04

Finish

Buttons, lining, press. Collected from the rail or sent on.
